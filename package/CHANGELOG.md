# Changelog

## v1

- Initial release with twenty weapons, each marked **(!)**:
  - **Melee:** Yari (spear blade swung overhead), Tanto, Wakizashi, Katana and Odachi (the Mastaba
    blade at each real sword's size, a ladder from fastest/shortest to slowest/longest; the Katana
    dashes 40 m on a full charge, the Odachi's full charge does 9999), Mjolnir (a thunderclap on
    every hit; a full charge throws it end over end, throwing chain lightning as it flies out and
    back), Warhammer (500-damage blow, shockwave, flings enemies up to 12 m, and walls hurt them), Gravity Hammer
    (a charge held forever drags enemies in; releasing a full charge blasts them out), Shadow Dagger
    (a fully charged hit makes you unseen for 6 s; kills while hidden add +25% damage up to +100%,
    full-windup kills stretch the stealth by the windup; putting it away ends it).
  - **Guns (infinite ammo):** Plasma Pistol/Rifle (Doom plasma), Hive Pistol/Rifle (Titanfall smart
    rounds), Laser Pistol/Rifle (continuous burning beams), Tentacle Pistol/Rifle (striker tentacles
    in a wide, level fan), Railgun (hitscan slug with infinite penetration and a double-helix beam),
    Chain Lightning Gun (bolts that jump between up to six enemies), Flamethrower (a short cone of
    fire with heavy range falloff, stacking afterburn that outlives the stream, fire that spreads
    between enemies standing close together, and an Aim-triggered fuel bomb for reach), Flak Cannon
    (Unreal Tournament style: bouncing shrapnel shards, or hold Aim to fire one ball that explodes
    into a radial shrapnel burst on contact), C-Foam Pistol/Rifle (the C-Foam Launcher's hardening
    foam sprayed full-auto - barely any damage, but enough stacks holds an enemy in place).
  - **Tools:** Grenade Launcher (the C-Foam launcher firing lock-melter grenades that blow on an
    enemy or bounce and blow after 3 s, flashing red faster and faster; infinite ammo), Victory
    Banner (set it down like a turret, pick it up like one too; heals everyone in its ring and buffs
    the holder's own damage +15%, climbing to +25% with kills made without leaving).
- Swap between this mod's weapons in a level with Page Up / Page Down (configurable).
- Config: per-weapon `BladeLength`, `BladeLengthAxis` and `Reach`; `[Gear Cycling]` keys;
  `[Tentacles] PoolMultiplier` raises how many tentacles the game can draw at once.
