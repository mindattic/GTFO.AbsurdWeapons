# Changelog

## v1

- Initial release with seventeen weapons, each marked **(!)**:
  - **Melee:** Katana (the Mastaba blade at twice a real katana's length; a full charge dashes up to
    10 m with the swing held, cutting the moment you reach an enemy or a wall), Mjolnir (a thunderclap on
    every hit; a full charge throws it end over end, throwing chain lightning as it flies out and
    back), Warhammer (500-damage blow, shockwave, flings enemies up to 12 m, and walls hurt them), Shadow Dagger
    (a fully charged hit makes you unseen for 6 s; kills while hidden add +25% damage up to +100%,
    full-windup kills stretch the stealth by the windup; putting it away ends it).
  - **Guns (infinite ammo):** Plasma Pistol/Rifle (Doom plasma), Hive Pistol/Rifle (Titanfall smart
    rounds, with lock-on markers), Laser Pistol/Rifle (continuous burning beams), Tentacle Cannon (striker tentacles
    spewed in a tight cone ahead), Railgun (a slow, devastating hitscan slug with infinite penetration and a double-helix beam),
    Chain Lightning Gun (bolts that jump between up to six enemies), Grenade Pistol/Rifle (lobbed lock-melter grenades that blow on
    an enemy or bounce and blow after 2.5 s, flashing red faster and faster).
  - **Tool:** Victory
    Banner (plant a flag with a glowing healing aura, pick it back up; heals everyone in its aura and buffs
    the holder's own damage +15%, climbing to +25% with kills made without leaving).
  - **Consumable:** Hand Grenade (a lock melter thrown like a glowstick that bounces and blows after
    3 s; every player starts each level with 99, and they never run out).
- Swap between this mod's weapons in a level with Page Up / Page Down (configurable).
- Config: per-weapon `BladeLength`, `BladeLengthAxis` and `Reach`; `[Gear Cycling]` keys;
  `[Tentacles] PoolMultiplier` raises how many tentacles the game can draw at once.
